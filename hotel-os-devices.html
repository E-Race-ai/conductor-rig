Mobile DevicesThe handhelds, tablets and mobile app staff use to run Hotel.OS on shift

One rugged 5G device per role replaces the radio, the pager and the paper task list. Each model is chosen for the work it does: thermal imaging for engineering, one-key push-to-talk for hands-busy roles, a touchscreen queue for front-line staff and a tablet board for supervisors. This sub-project owns the mobile layer end to end: device selection, the Hotel.OS mobile app on each form factor, offline sync, push-to-talk, location and NFC against the digital twin, identity and security, connectivity, charging and custody.

5 device models · 1 Android app · 7 departments · 1 MDM · ≈ 22-device pilot kit (est.)
Parent: PRJ-03 Hotel.OS · Sites: The Grand, then Hilton Thesis

Device profiles

What each handheld is for, what Hotel.OS puts on its screen, what it needs around it and what to watch for. Hardware facts are vendor-stated; items marked verify are checked on evaluation units at D0.

Engineering · security leadsSonim XP Pro ThermalRugged smartphone · 6.58″ · thermal
Why this device
Engineers diagnose with heat: a radiometric FLIR Lepton 3.5 shows a failing breaker, a blocked coil or a leak behind a wall before anything is opened. IP68 / IPX9K and a 2 m drop rating suit plant rooms and roofs.
On screen
Work-order queue sorted by SLA; asset card from the twin (circuit, last service); walk-to map; capture thermal and visible photos onto the work order; PTT to the engineering group; SOS.
Around it
Qi and cable charging; holster; engineering office charging rack.
Watch for
How thermal images reach the app: vendor SDK, camera intent or file share (verify). NFC presence (verify).
Housekeeping · F&B runners · patrolSonim XP5plus 5GPTT-first handset · 2.8″ · keypad
Why this device
Replaces the two-way radio for staff whose hands are busy: a side PTT key, top SOS and channel knobs work with gloves on. Removable 3,500 mAh battery for double shifts. Class I Div 2 rating.
On screen
Compact layout: one alert at a time (task, location, ETA), accept and complete with hardware keys, text directions instead of a full map, PTT group by floor or department.
Around it
Spare batteries for 24/7 roles; belt clip; housekeeping office rack.
Watch for
Runs Sonim OS on AOSP Android 15: Google services and Android Enterprise enrolment not stated. Push must not depend on Google services (HD-REQ-014).
Front desk · F&B · supervisorsRhino Mobility C65Enterprise handset · 6″ · Android 16
Why this device
Full touchscreen at a moderate price for the roles that create and route most tasks. Android 16 with AER, OEMConfig and zero-touch makes it the easiest model to manage at volume.
On screen
Full Hotel.OS handset layout: queue, create task, assign people or robots, track a W3 delivery, guest-request loop, floor map and walkthrough.
Around it
POGO cradle at the front desk; rack charging elsewhere; NFC badge sign-in.
Watch for
Non-removable 4,000 mAh battery (C65R variant for 24/7); Wi-Fi 4 only, so roaming must be tested.
Managers · front office · eventsRhino Mobility T105Rugged tablet · 11″ · 7,000 mAh
Why this device
Supervisors need the whole board at once. An 11″ screen shows rooms by status, staff, robots and SLAs side by side; eSIM keeps it online in event spaces.
On screen
Supervisor console: live board, reassignment, inspection checklists, SLA dashboards, robot fleet status, event set-up plans on the twin.
Around it
POGO dock at supervisor desks; rugged case for banquets.
Watch for
IP52 only: keep out of wet areas. 32 GB storage plus microSD; offline twin tiles need a size budget.
Shared infrastructureSonim MegaConnect5G hotspot · Wi-Fi 6/7 · 2.5 GbE
Why this device
Covers places where hotel Wi-Fi does not reach (garage, stairwells, loading dock) and gives the on-prem edge a cellular uplink if the property WAN fails.
On screen
No Hotel.OS screen; health and data use reported to the fleet board through the MDM.
Around it
Mains power where fixed; removable 8,000 mAh battery for pop-up event use.
Watch for
Carrier plan and data cap; physical security of fixed units.

Hotel.OS mobile app architecture

One native Android app with three layouts, built offline-first. Every device talks to the Hotel.OS edge on the property; the cloud is not in the critical path for taking or closing a task.

LayerDecisionReason
PlatformNative Android (Kotlin, Jetpack Compose), one codebaseAll five devices run Android or AOSP; no iOS in the fleet
LayoutsCompact keypad (XP5plus) · handset (XP Pro Thermal, C65) · tablet (T105)Same task model, different screen and input
OfflineEncrypted local store of the user's queue, nearby assets and floor tiles; outbox for every actionBasements, stairwells and elevators lose signal
Sync rulesServer decides assignment; device decides completion time and evidence; conflicts shown to the supervisorNo silent loss of work done offline
PushPersistent connection to the edge; Firebase only as an extra path where Google services existXP5plus runs AOSP, where Google services may be absent
Push-to-talkCarrier Enhanced PTT on Sonim handsets; app-level PTT over Wi-Fi on Rhino; groups follow Hotel.OS teams and floorsOne talk group per crew, set by the roster, not by hand
EvidencePhoto, thermal image, note or signature attached to the task on closeCloses the loop for guests, owners and audits
UpdatesApp and OS updates staged through the MDM: pilot ring, then propertyNo update lands mid-shift unannounced
