Job 2

Guest delivery

The Keenon W3 carries small items to guest doors in locked compartments. A smart cooler in the lobby, run by the front desk, is its pantry.

  1. RequestGuest asks by app, phone or at the desk; Hotel.OS creates the order.
  2. PickFront desk takes the items from the lobby cooler, which logs them automatically.
  3. DeliverThe W3 rides the lift, rings the room, and the guest's PIN opens the compartment.
  4. PayCard, or room charge with approval above a set limit. Alcohol always goes with a person.
  5. BrandEach W3 can carry the hotel's identity or a sponsor's wrap.
