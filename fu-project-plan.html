Tests

Test procedures

8.1Load test

  1. Complete the pre-run checklist (§8.2).
  2. On the Jetson: ~/loadtest/run.sh 300 (3600 for the soak test). Prints the output directory.
  3. Analyse: python3 ~/loadtest/an.py <dir> — per stream: fps, dropped frames (sequence gaps), MB/s, GB/h, frame interval p50/p99/max; system: CPU, RAM, junction temperature, VDD_IN.
  4. Add the result as a new row in §6.1 with date, setup and power source.
  5. Delete large output files (x4.mjpeg ≈ 2.5 GB per 5 min).

Harness: v4l2-ctl --stream-mmap per /dev/cam_*; tegrastats 1 s; df 10 s. Modes: Pocket 3 3840×2160, X4 2880×1440, BRIO 1920×1080, MJPEG 30 fps (edit run.sh).

8.2Pre-run checklist

  • ☐ Power source and level recorded; Omnicharge at 19 V.
  • ☐ nvpmodel -q → MAXN_SUPER.
  • ☐ ls /dev/cam_* lists every camera in use. Pocket 3: Webcam selected on its screen after any power loss.
  • ☐ lsusb -t: link speed per camera noted.
  • ☐ df -h: free space ≥ planned duration × §6.3 rate.
  • ☐ Time sync running (from WP6).
