PRJ-02 · Site Capture · Digital twin & Building Ledger · Rev C · 2026-11-24

Building LedgerThe located, verifiable record that Hotel.OS runs on

Captures (rig or phone video) are reconstructed and registered into one building frame. The building, its spaces, assets, tasks and every change are recorded in an append-only, hash-chained ledger. Hotel.OS reads and writes it through a located API, and staff, robots and integrations stay in sync over a live event stream. Everything on this page was measured on the bench building on 2026-11-23. Camera-only geometry is not survey grade; LiDAR (Mid-360S) is next.

Imagery of the bench building is withheld from this page. The working twin runs on the local network.

Ledger

Integrity model

PropertyImplementation
Append-onlyevents.jsonl; nothing is edited or deleted; corrections are new events
Tamper-evidenteach event hashes its content and the previous hash; edit, delete and reorder are detected (tested)
Signedevery event carries author and UTC time; edits refused without an author (SSO pending)
Stable IDsSITE / BLD / LVL / SPC / AST / TSK / ISS / CAP; never reused, retired assets keep history
Provenanceeach capture records source sha256, method and registration quality; each derived file is stored immutably by sha256; later versions supersede, never overwrite
Validationunknown parents, unlocated tasks, invalid task transitions and duplicate PMS room numbers are rejected and never written
